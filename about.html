About Us


AL JIBAL is a client-centered clearing and forwarding company that is passionate about serving its clients with excellence. We provide end-to-end logistics and clearing services, ensuring seamless flow and management of consignment, warehousing, processing, deployment, transportation, tracking, and storage.

Our services are tailor-made to suit our clients’ needs, rendered with diligence and passion. We stay proactive and responsive, ensuring the efficient transfer of goods and equipment globally and within Uganda.
