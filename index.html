Why Choose Us

Tailor made solutions

We understand that every business has unique requirements when it comes to logistics. That's why we offer tailor-made solutions that are designed to fit your specific needs.

Experience, efficiency and excellence

With years of experience in the logistics industry, Al Jibal has honed its expertise to deliver exceptional service and unparalleled efficiency.

Long term – relationships

Building strong and enduring relationships with our clients is a cornerstone of our business philosophy. We believe in forging partnerships based on trust, transparency, and open communication.

Freight Forwarding

We offer international and domestic air, road, ocean freight forwarding services. Handling small to large shipments with professionalism, we strive to meet all our clients' global freight forwarding needs within Uganda and around the world. Our approach is to provide tailored solutions that are built around the clients’ specific requirements.

Customs clearing

Our team is able to deal with all types of customs clearance at ports of entry and exit. We take care of all relevant documentation, customs examination, facilitate payments of duty, and arrange delivery once the cargo has been cleared.

Transit cargo management

We guarantee speedy and safe passage of goods through Uganda to other tax jurisdictions. Our company arranges transit bonds and enables duty-free transit of goods through Uganda so the cargo can reach international destinations hassle-free.
